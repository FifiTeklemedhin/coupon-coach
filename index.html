How this works, and what it can't do

Where the deals come from

Every Sunday a script reads CVS's weekly ad, rebuilds this deal list and checks the math. Anything it can't read clearly is held for a person to review before the update goes live. Categories are matched to CVS's site filters. The ad can vary a little by store, so check prices on your shelf. The banner at the top shows which ad week you're looking at, and it turns red once that week is over.

What it can't see

Your personal coupons and your ExtraBucks balance are in your account, which I can't open for you. The plan assumes the digital coupons it lists are available to you. If one isn't in your app, the cash for that step goes up, so check the number at the register.

Rules baked into the math

  • You can use ExtraBucks you earned on a later transaction. Rewards from a transaction can't be used on that same one, so each step pays with what the previous steps earned.
  • Guides describe a "98% rule": if coupons drop your total below a spend threshold, the reward may not print. Play it safe is on by default for that reason.
  • ExtraBucks are store credit, not cash. If a coupon or reward is worth more than the items, the extra isn't paid back.
  • ExtraBucks expire. Check the date on your receipt or in the app.
  • Tax is a rough estimate at the rate you enter.

If something goes wrong

If a reward doesn't print, don't buy the next step. Take your receipt to the register or customer service the same day. The coach re-plans from the ExtraBucks balance you actually have.

Not covered

Items where the ad shows no ExtraBucks amount, products in a group deal that the ad lists without a price, and gift-card deals are left out of plans.
